Gabriel Quezada Mata

Geographer, Universidad Austral de Chile. Valdivia, Chile.

I use satellite imagery, machine learning and GIS to study how land changes, and cartography to communicate the results.

I currently work as a geospatial data analyst for the FONDEF IDeA project SIS-CoSu (ID26I10080) at Universidad Austral de Chile: multitemporal land cover classification of Valdivia with Landsat, Sentinel-2 and a U-Net, reconstruction of 1990s land cover and simulation of future scenarios. I have also built accessibility indicators for every municipality in Chile, carried out GNSS surveys and drawn maps for foundations and cultural projects.

Tools: Python, QGIS, ArcGIS, R, Illustrator and Photoshop.
