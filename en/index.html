Geographer | Valdivia, Chile

Gabriel Quezada Mata

I am a geographer from Universidad Austral de Chile. I use satellite imagery, machine learning and GIS to understand how land changes, and cartography to make those results clear.

Projects (Spanish)Contact

What I do

  • Remote sensing: Landsat, Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 time series; land use and land cover (LULC) classification with Random Forest, XGBoost and U-Net.
  • Accessibility analysis: travel times over OpenStreetMap road networks on H3 hexagons; municipal indicators for all of Chile.
  • Cartography: technical maps, field maps, posters and illustrated maps.
  • Fieldwork and data: GNSS surveys, spatial databases and Python automation.

Current work

Geospatial data analyst at Universidad Austral de Chile for the FONDEF IDeA project SIS-CoSu (ID26I10080): multitemporal land cover classification of Valdivia with a U-Net, reconstruction of 1990s land cover and simulation of future scenarios.
